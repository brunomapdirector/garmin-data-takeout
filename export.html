Step 1 of 3 · Your key

Paste your Personal API key

You have an unfinished export. Paste your key and it continues where it stopped.

Get a key (2 minutes)

  1. Sign in to Garmin Connect on the web and open the Personal API Key page (Garmin's instructions).
  2. Click Create Key and accept the terms.
  3. Choose 30 days. You can delete it right after.
  4. Copy the key. Garmin shows it only once.

How your key is handled. It stays in this tab and disappears when you close it. It passes our relay once to get a 24 hour pass from Garmin. Nothing is stored or logged. Read the relay's code.
