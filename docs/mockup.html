Garmin Data Takeout

All your Garmin data, in one download

Activities with their original files, sleep, heart rate, stress, weight and more. Free, open source, no account needed.

Built on Garmin's official Personal API, so it does not break when Garmin changes its login.

Start my export View the code on GitHub

How it works

1

Create a key

In Garmin Connect, make a Personal API key. It takes two minutes and we show you where to click.

2

Paste it here

Choose what to include and from which date. Your key stays in your browser tab.

3

Download your zip

We fetch everything from Garmin and pack it into one file. Then you delete the key.

What you get

Everything Garmin's Personal API offers:

  • Every activity, with its original FIT file
  • A spreadsheet of all activities
  • Daily steps, calories and stress
  • Sleep and Body Battery
  • HRV and heart rate zones
  • Weight and hydration
  • VO2 max and fitness age
  • The raw data, untouched, for developers

The spreadsheets open in Excel, Numbers and Google Sheets. The FIT files import into Strava and most training apps.

Your key stays yours

Only in your tab

The key is never saved. Close the tab and it is gone.

Used once

Garmin's servers do not accept requests from websites directly, so a small relay passes them on. Your key goes through it once to get a 24 hour pass, and nothing is stored or logged.

Open code

The relay is under 100 lines. Read it on GitHub, and check the version shown at the bottom of every page.

Prefer to run it yourself?

Everything is public on GitHub. Your own copy means your key only touches computers you control.

Your own copy online

One click puts the whole site on your own free Cloudflare account.

Deploy to Cloudflare

On your computer

Needs Node.js. Then open the address it prints.

git clone github.com/…/garmin-data-takeout
cd garmin-data-takeout
npm install

Questions

Is it free?Yes. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Is this made by Garmin?No. It uses the Personal API that Garmin offers to every user.

How long does it take?A few minutes for a year of data, about 15 for ten years.

Why not Garmin's own export?You can use that too. It is emailed after a few days; this is right away and you choose what to include.

  3. Choose 30 days. You can delete it right after.
